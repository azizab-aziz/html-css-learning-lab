Notes and abbreviations

SKU
Stock Keeping Unit: the unique code that identifies a product.
TND
Tunisian dinar. All prices and values in this report use this currency.
Stock value
Quantity multiplied by unit price.
Reorder level
The quantity at which a product should be ordered again.
Reorder status
"OK" means the quantity is at or above the reorder level. "Reorder" means it is below. "Out of stock" means the quantity is 0.
