Warehouse Procedures

This page document core warehouse procedures: available feature, how products are received, how categories are organized, and key terms used across the system.

Warehouse Features

  • Real-time inventory tracking
  • Barcode scanning for received items
  • Automated low-stock alerts
  • Category-based product organization

Receiving a Product

  1. Scan the incoming item's barcode
  2. Verify the quantity against the purchase order
  3. Assign a shelf location
  4. Update the stock count in the system
