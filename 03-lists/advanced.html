Receiving Procedure

  1. Scan the incoming item's barcode
  2. Verify the quantity against the purchase order
  3. Assign a shelf location
  4. Update the stock count in the system

Stock Movement Procedure

  1. Identify the item and its current location
  2. Scan the item at pickup
  3. Transport the item to the new location
  4. Scan the item at drop-off to confirm the move
