Warehouse Operations Manual

A reference guide covering receiving, stock movement, safety, roles, and key terminology.

Receiving Procedure

  1. Scan the incoming item's barcode
  2. Verify the quantity against the purchase order
  3. Assign a shelf location
  4. Update the stock count in the system

Stock Movement Procedure

  1. Identify the item and its current location
  2. Scan the item at pickup
  3. Transport the item to the new location
  4. Scan the item at drop-off to confirm the move

Safety Checklist

  • Wear protective footwear at all times
  • Keep aisles clear of obstructions
  • Report damaged shelving immediately
  • Use proper lifting technique for heavy items

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Receiving Department
    • Verify incoming shipments
    • Log new stock into the system
  • Inventory Department
    • Monitor stock levels
    • Flag items for reorder

Glossary

SKU
A unique code used to identify a product.
Stock level
The quantity currently available.
Reorder threshold
The stock level at which new inventory should be ordered.
