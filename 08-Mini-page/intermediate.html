Warehouse product Management System

StockFlow helps a warehouse keep its stock records accurate. Every delivery, move, and shipment is recorded once, so the system always matches what is on the shelf.

It is built for small and medium warehouse that want clear numbers without a complicated setup.

Features

  • Real-time stock levels for every product
  • Barcode scanning when goods are received
  • Shelf location for every item
  • Automatic alerts when stock run low
  • Monthly inventory reports
