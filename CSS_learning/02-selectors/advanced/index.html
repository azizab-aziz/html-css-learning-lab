Operation Manual

Procedures every team member follows, from the dock to the shelf.

Receiving Procedure

Follow these steps for every delivery, in this order.

  1. Scan the delivery barcode
  2. Check the quantity against the purchase order
  3. Inspect for damage
    • Crushed boxes
    • Wet packaging
  4. Assign a shelf Location

Log each delivery as soon as it arrives.

Safety checklist

  • Keep aisles clear
  • Use proper lifting technique
