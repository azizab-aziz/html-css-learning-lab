Warehouse Management System

A short introduction to how warehouses keep track of their products.

What is warehouse management?

A warehouse management system helps businesses track products, mange where they are stored, and organize them by category.

It truns a pile of boxes into data: every item has a SKU, a quantity, and a shelf location.

Why is inventory tracking important?

Accurate inventory data reduces mistakes. When the system matches the shelf, orders ship on time staff stop wasting hours on recounts.
