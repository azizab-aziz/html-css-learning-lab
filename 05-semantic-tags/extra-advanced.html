Guide to cycle counting

Published 28 October 2026 by the inventory team

  • Why account regular
  • How a count works
  • Handling differences

A cycle count checks a small part of the warehouse each day instead of closing everything for one big annual acount. Errors are found while they are still and easy to trace.

How a count works

  1. Choose the self or product group to count today
  2. Count the physical items without looking at the system quantity
  3. Compare your acount with the number in the system
  4. Record the result and report any difference

Handling differences

When the acount and the system disagree, do not change the number straight away. First recount the item and check recent movements. Common causes:

  • A delivery that was received but not logged
  • An item moved to another shelf without a scan
  • A damaged item removed from stock

Common question

How often should we count?

Fast-moving and high-value products are counted more often, such as weekly. Slow-moving products can be counted monthly or quarterly.

Do we stop operations during a count?

No. count the shelves that are quite at that moment and leave busy ones for later in the day.
