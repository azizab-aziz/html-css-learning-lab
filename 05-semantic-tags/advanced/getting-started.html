Before you begin

What you need

A workstation with the system installed, a barcode scanner, and your staff account details.

Who to ask for help

Your shift supervisor can answer questions about accounts and equipment. Technical problems go to the contact page.

Your first delivery

  1. Scan the barcode on the incoming item
  2. Check the quantity against the purchase order
  3. Assign a shelf location
  4. Confirm the entry in the system

Tip

Log each delivery as soon as it arrives. Waiting until the end of the shift is how records drift out of date.
