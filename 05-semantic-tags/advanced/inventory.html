Tracking stock

Stock levels

Each product has a stock level: the quantity currently available on the shelves.

Stock alerts

When a product falls below its reorder threshold, the system raises an alert so new stock can be ordered in time.

Keeping data accurate

Accurate records depend on updating the system after every movement. A scan at pickup and a scan at drop-off keeps each item's location correct.

Good to know
