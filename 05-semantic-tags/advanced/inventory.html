Tracking stock

Stock levels

Each product has a stock level: the quantity currently available on the shelves.

Stock alerts

When a product falls below its reorder threshold, the system raises an alert so new stock can be ordered in time.

Keeping data accurate

Accurate records depend on updating the system after every movement. A scan at pickup and a scan at drop-off keeps each item's location correct.

Frequently asked questions

what is a reorder threshold?

The quantity at which a product should be ordered again.Below it, the system shows an alert.

How often should I update the stock count?

After every movement, so the system always matches what is on the shelf.

What if the count on the shelf does not match the system?

Tell your supervisor and recount the item. Do not edit the number without checking it first.
